How we work

From recce to wrap

One team from start to finish, so nobody has to explain the shot twice.

STEP 1

Recce

We read the script pages, see the location or stage and agree on what the shot needs.

STEP 2

Engineering

Drawings, load calculations and a safety plan, signed off before a single piece moves.

STEP 3

Prep & rig

Gear from our warehouse, rigged and tested before the unit arrives.

STEP 4

On set

Our riggers stay with the unit and run every take until the last setup.

STEP 5

Strike

We strike, inspect the gear and hand the location back as we found it.

FAQ

Before you call

Do you work with foreign productions?

Yes. We work alongside the production service company and your heads of department, and our crew handles set communication in English.

Do you work outside Spain?

Yes. Our warehouse is in Madrid, and we travel with our gear and crew to locations across Spain and abroad.

How much notice do you need?

It depends on the rig. Standard camera and set rigging can be ready in days; a custom stunt or flying rig needs time for engineering and rehearsals with the stunt team.

Who is responsible for rigging safety?

We are. Every rig comes with load calculations, inspected gear and a safety plan, and our riggers stay on set while it is in use. Insurance details are shared on request.
